This Millbrae hotel is located one mile from the San Francisco International Airport. They also feature a gym and spacious suites. San Francisco Airport Fairfield Inn suites are furnished with a separate seating area, microwave and refrigerator. Cable TV with in-room movies is also included. Fairfield Inn San Francisco Airport provides free Wi-Fi and a free hot buffet breakfast every morning. A 24-hour front desk and business services are available for added convenience. The Millbrae Bay Area Rapid Transit system (BART) is 10 minutes' walk from the hotel and provides easy access to downtown San Francisco. South San Francisco Conference Center is 10 minutes' drive from the San Francisco Fairfield Inn and Suites and Ocean Beach is 30 minutes' drive away.

Couples in particular like the location – they rated it 8.7 for a two-person trip.
